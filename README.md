# 📦 Supply Chain Inventory Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
Analysis of 125,000+ orders from a sports supply chain
to identify inventory performance and demand patterns.

## 🔍 Key Findings
- Only **9 products** generate **80% of total sales** ($24.64M)
- Remaining products sold **1,000 units or less** = dead stock
- Demand **peaks in March & October** — drops in February
- **Cleats** is the top category with 60,000+ units sold

## 💡 Recommendations
- Focus inventory investment on the 9 high-moving products
- Reduce dead stock to cut unnecessary storage costs
- Build up stock before March and October
- Review pricing on low-margin products

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power Query** — Data Cleaning
- **DAX** — KPI & Pareto Calculations
- **Power BI** — Dashboard & Visualizations
